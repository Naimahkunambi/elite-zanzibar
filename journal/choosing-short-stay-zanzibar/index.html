How to Choose the Right Short Stay in Zanzibar

Bedroom count is only the beginning. Use this practical checklist to choose a Zanzibar stay around location, comfort, trip type and everyday logistics.

A beautiful room is only one part of a good stay. The best accommodation is the one that fits the way you will actually use Zanzibar day to day.

Start with the travelling party

A one-bedroom stay can be ideal for a solo traveller or couple, while families and groups usually benefit from more private bedrooms and more generous shared space. Do not choose only by the number of people; think about sleeping arrangements, privacy and how much time everyone will spend together indoors.

Choose location around your itinerary

Zanzibar offers very different experiences across Stone Town, the west coast and the island’s beach destinations. If you expect to move around often, transport time matters. If you want to slow down, being close to your main beach, restaurant or activity area may be more important.

Look beyond the bedroom

  • Shared living space: especially important for families and groups.
  • Kitchen facilities: useful for longer stays or travellers who prefer flexibility with meals.
  • Air conditioning and ventilation: confirm what is available in the specific unit.
  • Wi-Fi: ask about availability if you will work or rely heavily on connectivity.
  • Cleaning arrangements: understand what is included and what can be scheduled separately.
  • Transport: consider airport pickup and planned transfers before arrival.

Ask what is actually confirmed

Photos can help you understand style and layout, but the booking decision should be based on confirmed information for the exact accommodation: bedroom configuration, amenities, available dates, rate, payment process and any booking conditions.

Think about the whole stay, not just check-in

A smooth trip also depends on the practical moments around the accommodation. Airport movement, cleaning during a longer stay, or a maintenance issue can matter just as much as the room itself. Choosing a provider that can coordinate those needs can reduce the number of separate contacts you have to manage.

A simple decision framework

  • Who is travelling?
  • Where will you spend most of your time?
  • How much shared space do you need?
  • Which amenities are essential rather than nice to have?
  • How will you get from the airport and around the island?
  • What information must be confirmed before you commit?
