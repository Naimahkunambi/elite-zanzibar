Best Time to Visit Zanzibar: Weather, Seasons & Planning

Understand Zanzibar’s dry months and two rainy seasons, then choose travel dates around the experience you actually want.

There is no single perfect month for every Zanzibar trip. The right time depends on how much you value dry weather, quieter travel periods, outdoor plans and flexibility.

Zanzibar has two rainy seasons

The Tanzania Meteorological Authority classifies the Zanzibar Islands within a bimodal rainfall zone. The long rains, known as Masika, typically run from March through May. The shorter Vuli rains generally occur from October through December.

TMA also notes that January and February are relatively dry in bimodal areas, while June through September are generally dry months across Tanzania. Actual rainfall varies from year to year, so seasonal patterns are a planning guide rather than a guarantee.

June to September: a strong choice for outdoor plans

The June-to-September dry period is useful when your itinerary depends heavily on walking, beach time or moving between different areas. Even then, tropical weather can shift quickly, so keep some flexibility in day-by-day plans.

January and February: another relatively dry window

TMA describes January and February as relatively dry for areas with two rainy seasons. These months can work well for travellers who want warm coastal weather outside the main June-to-September dry period.

March to May: plan for Masika

The long-rain period can bring heavier and more persistent rainfall. If you travel then, choose accommodation you will genuinely enjoy spending time in and avoid building an itinerary that depends on every day being clear.

October to December: expect more changeable days

The Vuli short rains often arrive during these months. They do not necessarily mean continuous rain, but visitors should plan with a little more flexibility and monitor short-range forecasts closer to the trip.

Choose dates around your priorities

  • For maximum weather reliability: start by considering the drier periods.
  • For a flexible, slow-paced stay: shoulder and rainy periods can still work if your plans are adaptable.
  • For families or groups: prioritize comfortable indoor space and simple transport arrangements whatever the season.
  • For a longer stay: choose accommodation that still works well on days when you do not want to go out.
