Privacy Policy

How ELITE handles personal information submitted through this website.

Effective 5 November 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be handled when you use the ELITE website, prepare an enquiry, follow a configured contact link, or communicate with ELITE about accommodation or services.

2. Information you may provide

  • Name and contact details such as a telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Travel or stay details, including preferred dates, guest count and bedroom requirements.
  • Service information, including location, requested work and transport details.
  • Any other information you choose to include in an enquiry.

3. How the current website handles form data

The website currently prepares enquiry information in your browser. It does not transmit the form to an ELITE server by itself. If WhatsApp or email is configured and you choose that option, the information is handed to that external service for you to send. If you use the copy function, the text remains under your control until you paste or send it elsewhere.

4. Why information may be used

Information sent to ELITE may be used to respond to enquiries, assess availability or service scope, arrange requested services, maintain customer correspondence, prevent misuse and meet legal or operational obligations.

5. Sharing and service providers

ELITE may share personal information where reasonably necessary for the requested service, where required by law, or with service providers used to operate communications and business systems. Third-party services such as WhatsApp or email providers operate under their own privacy terms.

6. Retention and security

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, applicable legal requirements, record keeping and dispute management. ELITE will apply reasonable access controls and practical security measures to customer information it stores.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies to a particular processing activity, individuals may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or complaints about the handling of their personal information. Requests can be made through the Contact page once ELITE’s official contact details are configured.

8. Tanzanian and Zanzibar legal context

Tanzania’s Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 and the Personal Data Protection (Personal Data Collection and Processing) Regulations, 2023 establish personal-data principles and rights. The Act and Regulations expressly state that their Zanzibar application is limited in relation to non-Union matters. The exact legal requirements that apply can depend on the nature of the processing and the Zanzibar or Union-law context.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when ELITE adds new systems, analytics, payment tools, booking technology or other processing activities. The effective date should be updated whenever a material change is published.

2. Enquiries and confirmations

Availability, rates, exact accommodation, service scope, timing, transport arrangements and any payment terms become binding only when they are specifically confirmed by ELITE through an authorized communication channel.

3. Accommodation information

Bedroom configurations and representative imagery help visitors understand the type of stay they are looking for. The exact property, verified photographs, amenities, capacity, rate and booking conditions should be confirmed before a reservation is accepted.

4. Services

Cleaning, fumigation, maintenance, transport and logistics requests are subject to ELITE confirming the location, scope, access, timing, resources and any applicable price or service conditions.
